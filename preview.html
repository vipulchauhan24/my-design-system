Design system v0.1

Built around orange

One set of tokens for the portfolio, the Tailwind data grid and the UI library. An orange that passes AA on white, warm neutrals, and density you can turn up or down.

Explore components See the palette

Color

Seven hues on a 50-950 scale, tuned per hue. Ember 500 (#D14800) is the brand fill in light mode; ember 400 (#FF671F) is the brand on dark. The rest are derived to sit beside them.

Feedback
